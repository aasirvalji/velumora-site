For App Review

Launch Velumora to begin interacting immediately. Studio contains World, Color and Motion. Save compositions from the canvas, then reopen or manage them in Collection. Sharing renders a high-resolution PNG before presenting the native iOS share sheet.

Photos permission is requested only if Save Image is chosen. Settings provides visual quality, haptics, motion preferences, Privacy Policy and Support.

Saved worlds preserve their parameters and ambient phase. Temporary touch forces and particle bursts are not retained. The app has no audio or video export feature. This page's video is a screen recording.
